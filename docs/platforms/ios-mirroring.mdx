---
title: iOS through iPhone Mirroring
description: Drive an iPhone through the macOS iPhone Mirroring window, with no developer mode.
---

macOS 15 can mirror a nearby iPhone into a window. agentthumbs captures that
window and moves the Mac's pointer and keyboard over it, so nothing runs on
the iPhone and Developer Mode is not needed.

<Warning>
Apple does not offer iPhone Mirroring in the European Union. Use
[WebDriverAgent](/platforms/ios-wda) there.
</Warning>

## Requirements

- macOS 15 or newer and an iPhone on iOS 18 or newer, signed in to the same
  Apple Account
- The iPhone Mirroring app open and connected to the iPhone
- Screen Recording and Accessibility permission for the app that runs
  agentthumbs (your terminal, or your MCP client), in System Settings →
  Privacy & Security

`agentthumbs devices` lists what is missing when the iPhone does not
appear.

## Limits

- One iPhone per Mac.
- The pointer moves for each action and is put back afterwards. Avoid using
  the mouse while the agent works.
- There is no accessibility tree. Elements come from text that Apple's
  Vision OCR reads on the screen, so icons without a label have no number
  and the agent targets them by pixel.
- `open_app` takes the app's name as shown on the Home Screen, not a bundle
  id. `list_apps` and `push_media` are not available.
- Keys: `home`, `app_switch`, `enter` and `delete`.

## The helper

A small Swift helper does the capture and the input. agentthumbs ships it as
a universal binary in `@appeeky/agentthumbs`. Set `AGENTTHUMBS_MIRRORING_HELPER` to
use a helper at another path.
