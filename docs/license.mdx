## What you can do

The license allows any purpose other than a competing use, and names these
explicitly:

- **Internal use.** Run agentthumbs for yourself or inside your company, on
  your own phones and accounts, in your own scripts, agents and internal
  tools.
- **Professional services.** Use it in work you do for a client who uses
  agentthumbs under these terms.
- **Non-commercial education and research.**

You can also copy, modify and redistribute it for those purposes, as long as
the license and copyright notices travel with it.

## What you can't do without a commercial license

A **competing use** is making agentthumbs available to others in a commercial
product or service that:

1. substitutes for agentthumbs,
2. substitutes for another product or service Appeeky offers using
   agentthumbs, that exists when the version is released, or
3. offers the same or substantially similar functionality as agentthumbs.

In practice: selling agentthumbs, hosting it as a phone-automation service for
others, or building a product whose features are agentthumbs' features.

## Commercial licenses

If you want to build agentthumbs into a product or service you offer to
others, [contact Appeeky](https://appeeky.com) for a commercial license.

## Every version becomes MIT

Each version is also licensed under the MIT license from the second
anniversary of its release. A version released in October 2026 can be used
under MIT from October 2028, with no restrictions beyond MIT's.
